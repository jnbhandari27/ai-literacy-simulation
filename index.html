Activity 2 · Understanding

Two Cases, Two Kinds of Decisions

Read each short case, decide what kind of decision it is, then see which parts of the AI's advice you could actually check yourself. Utilitarian decisions are about practical goals and facts; hedonic decisions are about taste, feeling and occasion. There's no penalty — every step ends with an explanation.

Side by side

Utilitarian decisions

What you're afterA practical goal with clear criteria — specs, price, time.
Can you check the AI's advice?Mostly yes. The claims are facts you can look up in minutes.
Who is the final judge?The facts.
How to use the AILet it shortlist quickly, then do a fast fact-check.

Hedonic decisions

What you're afterA feeling or experience — joy, warmth, meaning.
Can you check the AI's advice?Only partly. The facts can be checked, but the part that matters most depends on taste and emotion.
Who is the final judge?You — you know the people and the occasion.
How to use the AITake it as a source of ideas, then test them against what you know about the person and the moment.

Real decisions often mix both: a laptop can be exciting, and a dinner has a price. A good habit is to ask which part of the AI's advice you are relying on — the facts, or the feeling.
